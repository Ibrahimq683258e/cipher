Frequently asked questions

The short version of how Cipher handles your data and your workflow.

Are files uploaded?
No. Files and passwords are processed in your browser and are not sent to Cipher's backend.
What if I lose my password?
Cipher cannot recover it. AES-256-GCM requires the original password to decrypt your file.
Does Cipher save results?
Results stay in browser memory until downloaded or the page closes.
