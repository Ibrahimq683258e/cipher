Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Files and Tool Data

Cipher tools process your inputs in your browser and do not send selected files, passwords, or results to Cipher for processing. Your browser still connects to our website host and, where used, third-party services.

2. Browser Storage, Cookies, and Hosting

Cipher uses localStorage for interface preferences and to save your cookie choices. Necessary storage supports site features. If you allow advertising or analytics, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, browser or device details, pages visited, and interactions. Our host may also receive technical request data such as IP address, requested page, and request time.

3. Third-Party Services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile and sends messages to a Google Apps Script service. These providers receive information needed to deliver their services; see their privacy policies for details.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

Cipher displays advertisements from third-party advertising providers to support the service. If you allow advertising technologies in Cookie settings, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, or personalize ads. They may collect or receive your IP address, browser or device identifiers, pages viewed, and ad interactions. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies.

You can reject advertising technologies or change your choice at any time using Cookie settings. Depending on your location and the advertising configuration, additional provider controls or legally required notices may apply.

5. Analytics

When analytics are enabled, and only if you allow them in Cookie settings, analytics providers may collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location derived from your IP address, and browser or device details to help us understand visits and improve the site. Analytics technologies are optional and can be disabled at any time through Cookie settings.

6. Contact Messages and Privacy Requests

If you use the contact form, your name, email, message, and anti-bot verification information are sent to the configured services so we can respond. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. Use the contact form for questions or requests; available privacy rights depend on applicable law.
